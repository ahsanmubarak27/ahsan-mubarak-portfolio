Title:
Analysis & Key Findings

Present the actual Google Ads analysis clearly and visually.

KEY FUNNEL METRICS

Display the following values:

- Impressions: 11.8M
- Clicks: 361.2K
- CTR: 3.07%
- Leads: 52K
- Lead Conversion from Clicks: 14.4%
- Conversions: 16.9K
- Conversion Rate from Leads: 32.51%

Use clean metric cards or a compact visual hierarchy consistent with the existing case study design.

KEY FINDINGS

1. Funnel Performance

The largest drop-off occurs between impressions and clicks, indicating that getting users to engage with the advertisements is the primary challenge in the funnel.

2. Device Performance

Mobile devices show the highest CTR and conversion performance, indicating stronger engagement and conversion potential from mobile users.

3. Campaign Performance

Campaign performance varies across the available campaign and advertising dimensions, creating opportunities to refine targeting and identify stronger-performing segments.

4. Optimization Opportunity

Improving ad engagement at the impression-to-click stage while continuing to scale stronger-performing segments could improve overall campaign efficiency.
