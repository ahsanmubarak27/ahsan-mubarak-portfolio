03 — ANALYSIS & KEY FINDINGS
==================================================

Title:
Analysis & Key Findings

Present the following findings clearly and visually.

TOP FINDINGS

1. High-margin products stood out.

Decaf Espresso recorded the highest profit margin at 36.9%, followed by Darjeeling at 35.2% and Columbian at 33.9%.

2. Green Tea was a major profitability concern.

Green Tea recorded a -127.5% profit margin, making it the most significant low-margin product identified in the analysis.

3. Profitability varied across states.

California and Illinois were among the strongest contributors to overall profit, while New Mexico recorded substantially lower profit.

4. Major markets generated higher sales.

Major markets generated approximately $425.1K in sales compared with $394.7K from small markets.

5. Sales and profit were strongly related.

Sales and profit showed a strong positive correlation of 0.80, indicating that higher sales generally coincided with higher profit, although low-margin products could still reduce profitability.
