==================================================
14. PHOTO EDITING & VISUAL INTEGRATION
==================================================

The event photographs must be visually adapted to the existing website theme.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT simply place the raw uploaded photographs into the website.

Before displaying them, adjust their presentation so they naturally integrate with the portfolio's existing visual identity.

The website's existing visual identity is the primary reference:

- Dark
- Clean
- Minimalist
- Professional
- Precise
- Modern
- Premium
- Detail-oriented

PHOTO TREATMENT:

- Use appropriate cropping and positioning.
- Adjust the aspect ratio to fit the existing layout.
- Preserve the important subjects and context of the photographs.
- Use subtle contrast/exposure adjustments if necessary so the photographs work naturally against the dark website background.
- If appropriate, apply a very subtle dark overlay or tonal treatment to improve visual consistency.
- Keep the photographs natural and professional.
- Avoid excessive filters.
- Avoid artificial cinematic effects.
- Avoid excessive blur.
- Avoid heavy gradients.
- Avoid neon or futuristic effects.
- Do not make the photographs look heavily edited.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT alter, regenerate, replace, or significantly modify the people's faces or physical appearance.

The goal is not to change the photographs themselves, but to make their presentation consistent with the website.

The photographs should look like authentic documentation of the experience while still feeling visually integrated into a premium professional portfolio.

PHOTO HIERARCHY:

Photo 1:
Team photograph with the event division members.

This should emphasize:
- Leadership
- Team coordination
- Human connection

Photo 2:
Event execution photograph.

This should emphasize:
- Execution
- Event environment
- Leadership in practice

Use the strongest crop for each photograph.

Do not create a large gallery.

Two photographs are sufficient.

VISUAL CONSISTENCY:

The photographs should use the same:

- Border radius
- Border treatment
- Spacing
- Shadow treatment
- Hover behavior
- Animation style

already established elsewhere on the website.

Do not create a separate photography style that conflicts with the Hero, About Me, or Projects sections.

The final result should feel like:

"Real professional experience presented through the same visual identity as the rest of the portfolio."
