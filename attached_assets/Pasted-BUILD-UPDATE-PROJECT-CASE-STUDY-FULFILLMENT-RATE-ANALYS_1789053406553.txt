==================================================
FINDING 1 — NO CLEAR RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN LEAD TIME AND FULFILLMENT
==================================================

Title:
No Clear Relationship Between Lead Time and Fulfillment

Insight:

The scatter plot shows no clear trend or direction.

The correlation between Fulfillment Rate and Lead Time is weak and statistically insignificant.

This indicates that lead time is unlikely to be a major factor affecting fulfillment within the available data.

Use an appropriate scatter plot or existing project visualization.

==================================================
FINDING 2 — HIGH STOCK LEVELS DON'T ENSURE HIGH FULFILLMENT
==================================================

Title:
High Stock Levels Don't Ensure High Fulfillment

Insight:

No strong pattern was observed between stock levels and fulfillment rate.

Some high stock levels still result in low fulfillment rates.

Examples:

- Stock level 36 → fulfillment rate of 7.1%
- Stock level 80 → fulfillment rate of 10.4%

This suggests that having high stock levels alone does not guarantee strong fulfillment performance.

IMPORTANT:
Do not interpret this as proof that stock levels have no operational impact in general.

The correct conclusion is that the available data does not show a strong pattern between stock levels and fulfillment performance.

==================================================
FINDING 3 — SHIPPING TIME ALSO FAILS TO PREDICT FULFILLMENT
==================================================

Title:
Shipping Time Also Fails to Predict Fulfillment

Insight:

As with lead time, the data points are scattered without a clear trend.

There is no strong correlation between shipping time and fulfillment.

This indicates that shipping delays may not be the primary driver of low fulfillment within the available data.

==================================================
WHAT WE KNOW SO FAR
==================================================

Add a final analytical summary block.

Title:
What We Know So Far

Use:

No strong relationship was found between fulfillment and stock levels, lead time, or shipping time.

A likely contributing factor could be low warehouse or order processing utilization, but this could not be tested due to data limitations.

Further investigation is needed to confirm the underlying operational cause and guide improvements.

IMPORTANT:
